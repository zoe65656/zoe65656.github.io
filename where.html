Resources

Here is a recommendation of places to source sticker sheets from!

Local

Artist Alley Shop

Located on Yonge street, the Artist Alley Shop houses creations by local artists per shelf. A wide varation of artworks in forms of stickers, keychains, prints, and more can be bought. The Artist Alley Shop is a good source for many sorts of stickers sheets!

Art Markets

Many conventions or events include an artist alley, featuring dozens or hundreds of small business who sell a variety of items, including possibly sticker sheets!

Examples:

Mako Art Market

Toronto Stationery Show

Online

Etsy

An online marketplace with independent shops from across the globe. Hundreds of small artists use this platform to sell their creations, vintage stickers can be bought from collectors as well!

Instagram

Social media can be a good starting point to find artists who design their original sticker sheets. Browsing through hashtags is a good place to start, such as with #decosticker or #stickersheet. It can also be a good way to find out about local events/artists!
