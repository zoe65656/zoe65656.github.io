History of stickers

For many years, papers such as advertisements, notices, posters, and more, were stuck onto surfaces with adhesion. Stickers from the past required the user to moisten the back for the adhesive to activate.

The first self-adhesive stickers were created by R. Stanton Avery in 1935, with it's original use being for labeling goods and products. The invention of self-adhesive stickers allowed companies to create decorative stickers, such as Sanrio's Hello Kitty stickers in 1976.
During 2019-2020, the company StickerYou opened a store in Toronto which also featured a History of Stickers Museum.
