Style Date range

Descriptions of styles and dates
 

Deco

A decorative and detailed genre of sticker sheets. Usually contains small sized shapes (stars, sparkles, objects, etc)

Functional

Sticker sheets that are purposeful. Some common functional stickers are planner stickers, or stickers that can be used like a memo. Sheets with mainly square cuts are included as they are ideal for decorating planners.

Misc

Stickers that don't fit into an exact category.
