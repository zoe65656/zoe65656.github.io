Trends

Characteristics in past sticker sheets vary per region, however there is a distinct aged, or outdated look to them all.
A contributing factor is the use of gradients, whether for shading or as texture.

With improved and accessible technology, more people are able to create sticker designs of their own. With so many creators, the style of stickers are equally as diverse. A lot of sticker sheets are created around a mascot made by the artist, which gives the artist an identity/brand. There are trends that take place within the sticker sheet community, such as the rise of deco around the early 2020s, but currently it is very much mascot driven.
