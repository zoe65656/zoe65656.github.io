Why do people collect?

There are many reasons behind collecting stickers. For some, there is an aspect of fulfillment which comes from successfully hunting for a rare and vintage design, and building a collection of them. There are also those who simply find joy in buying, using and collecting stickers. Especially during the pandemic, engaging in an analogue and nostalgic hobby was a refreshing escape from the screens and the ongoing issues. Stickers create a feeling of joy and nostalgia, making it a cherished item to many.
